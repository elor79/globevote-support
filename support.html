Help & support.

GlobeVote is a conversation game for iPhone and iPad: ask anything, guess the majority, and see the answers on a glass globe. We answer every message to info@elorenz.ch within 24 hours, reports about content included.

How do I start?

Tap Ask, type a question, choose a style (pick one, pick several, rate on a scale, or open answers), optionally a topic (one of twelve, or your own), and create. Vote yourself, then pass the phone, start a nearby room, or publish to the world.

How does a nearby room work?

The host opens a poll and taps Start a nearby room. Friends choose Join nearby from the menu and pick the room; the host lets each guest in. Answers sync directly between the phones over an encrypted local connection. Keep GlobeVote open on all devices; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on; no internet or account needed. Up to eight devices; for bigger groups pass one phone around.

What is the World tab?

Polls that hosts chose to publish to the world. They live in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and are visible to every GlobeVote user. You need to be signed in to iCloud on your device to see them and to answer; each iCloud account can answer once per poll. Pull the refresh button to load the latest. Publishing is off by default and can be switched off again on the poll's screen, which also removes its world answers.

Why is half the globe dark?

The globe is lit by the real sun: it is night where it is night right now, and the city lights show there. Tap the sun icon at the top to change it: shift the clock, choose "Always day" or "Always night", thin the clouds, brighten the city lights, hide the moon or the glow, or stop the idle spin. "Back to the real sky" restores everything.

How do I move around the globe?

Drag to turn it, flick to let it spin, pinch to zoom, double-tap to zoom in on a spot or back out. Tap a marker or a region chip to open that region. The filter button in the bottom bar narrows the globe to a topic or to 18+ conversations; the magnifier searches the current tab; "Regions" lists every region and finds one by country name.

Where does a percentage come from?

Tap any result. GlobeVote shows the arithmetic (for example "6 answers ÷ 14 in scope = 42.86% → 43%"), what is counted, the split by region and every answer behind the figure. The chart chips switch between bars, a ring and a by-region view of the same numbers.

I saw something inappropriate

Open the poll's menu (⋯) and choose Report & hide. It disappears from your device immediately, and three reports from different accounts hide it for everyone. Block this host in the same menu hides everything that host publishes. For anything urgent, email us; we act within 24 hours.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a poll from its screen to remove it and its local answers. In How GlobeVote works (menu) tap Delete my world data to remove every poll, answer and report your iCloud account put on the world globe. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Why did a code not import?

Paste the whole GlobeVote link or GV1 code. A ballot only imports when its poll is already on your device, and a poll you reported is never imported again.

Does GlobeVote need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. The only account involved is your own iCloud account, and only if you use the World tab.
