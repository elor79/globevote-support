How do friends vote?

Create a poll and choose “Invite friends to answer.” Your friend opens the invitation in GlobeVote, saves a vote and chooses “Send my ballot.” Open their reply, preview it, and import it. Both devices need GlobeVote. There is no automatic synchronization.

Can we use one phone?

Yes. The host can choose “Pass the phone · another voter.” The next voter sees a handoff screen before answering. This is an informal group activity, not identity-verified voting.

Why is my vote not on the globe?

Regions are optional. Ballots without a region count in results but do not add globe markers. Markers represent broad regions, not precise locations.

How do I delete data?

Open a poll and choose “Delete from this device.” Shared copies remain with their recipients. See the privacy policy for backup details.

A code won’t import

Paste the entire GlobeVote invitation or ballot link. Ballots need their poll invitation on the receiving device first. Reimporting the same ballot does not add another vote.

Need more help?

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your iPhone/iPad model, iOS version, GlobeVote version and the steps that caused the issue.

GitHub requires an account to submit an issue. Requests are public: do not post private questions, ballot codes, personal details or other sensitive information.
