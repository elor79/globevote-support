GlobeVote is a conversation game for iPhone and iPad. Ask your circle anything, guess the majority, and explore answers on a glass globe. Publish a question to the world, if you like, and watch answers arrive by region.

A living Earth in your pocket

The globe is the real one: NASA imagery with mountains and deserts in relief, oceans that catch the light, clouds drifting on the winds, daylight and night following the actual sun, city lights on the dark side and the moon in tonight's phase. Spin it, flick it, tap a region to fly there, or set your own sky: shift the clock, thin the clouds, brighten the cities, hide the moon.

Every number can be opened: tap a result to see the arithmetic, the scope, and the answers behind it, then view the same data as bars, a ring or by region. Twelve topics or your own, filters, search, and a country finder for regions.

Made for your circle

Start an opt-in nearby room and answer together, or pass one phone around. Manual invitations remain available for distant friends. Clear percentages and optional broad regions turn every answer into a new perspective.

No accounts, ads, tracking or subscriptions. Nearby rooms share answers directly after you opt in. The world globe is opt in per poll and runs on Apple’s iCloud; unpublished polls never leave your device.
