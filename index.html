GlobeVote is an offline conversation game for iPhone and iPad. Ask your circle anything, guess the majority, and explore answers on an interactive globe.

Made for your circle

Pass one phone around, or share a poll invitation and exchange ballot replies. Clear percentages and optional broad regions turn every answer into a new perspective.

No accounts, ads, tracking, subscriptions or automatic uploads. Remote ballot exchange requires GlobeVote on each device and manual imports. There is no live worldwide feed.

Currently in pre-release development. App Store availability has not been announced.
