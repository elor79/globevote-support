What stays on your device

GlobeVote stores poll questions, answer options, ballots, optional majority predictions and optional broad regions in an app file on your device. The app does not operate its own server, create accounts, use analytics or advertising SDKs, or request your device location. connectix gmbh does not receive your polls or ballots through the app unless you publish a poll to the world globe (see below).

What you choose to share

You can share invitations, ballots or results images through the system share sheet. Invitations include question and answer wording. Ballots include a selected answer, optional prediction, optional broad region and random identifiers that connect the ballot to a poll and prevent duplicate imports. Shared codes are readable and are not encrypted. Recipients and your selected messaging service may retain shared content under their own policies. Nothing is uploaded to a developer-operated polling server. In an opt-in nearby room, the host explicitly admits guests and GlobeVote automatically exchanges the room’s question, answers, optional predictions and broad regions directly between connected devices. All admitted participants can receive the shared ballots. Connections require encryption. Nearby discovery advertises a random room code and random guest labels, not your device name or an account profile. Leave or end the room to stop sharing; already received copies remain with participants. The app requests Local Network permission only for this feature.

The world globe, if you opt in

Publishing is off by default. If you switch on “Publish to the world” for a poll, that question, its answer options, category, style and optional topic region are stored in Apple’s CloudKit public database for GlobeVote and shown to all GlobeVote users on the World tab. Every answer to a published poll, including its optional prediction and optional broad region, is stored there too and shown to all users in aggregate. Answers and reports are keyed to a one-way hash of your iCloud user record identifier so each iCloud account counts once; the hash is never displayed and connectix gmbh never receives your Apple ID, name or email. Unpublishing deletes the question and its world answers from CloudKit. Three reports from different accounts hide a poll for everyone automatically; there is no human moderator, but reports sent to info@elorenz.ch are handled within 24 hours. You can also block a host, which is stored only on your device. "Delete my world data" in the app deletes every poll, answer and report your account created. Apple processes this data under Apple’s iCloud terms, and connectix gmbh can view the public database through Apple’s CloudKit console to operate the service.

Backups and deletion

App files may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings. Deleting a poll removes its local ballots. Copies already shared and existing backups are not deleted by that action. Removing the app removes its local app container according to operating system behavior.

This website and support

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as your IP address to provide hosting under its privacy statement. These pages use no analytics, advertising scripts or contact forms. If you voluntarily post a support issue on GitHub, the issue is public and handled through GitHub. Do not include private poll content or personal information.

What we declare to Apple

On the App Store privacy label GlobeVote declares two data types, both only for the app to work and never for tracking or advertising: user content (the questions and answers you choose to publish) and a user ID (the hashed iCloud identifier that limits answers to one per account). Nothing is collected unless you publish or answer a world poll.
