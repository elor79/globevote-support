Standing on
public shoulders.

GlobeVote links no third-party library. The living Earth is built from public-domain science data, the app from Apple’s frameworks, and the project from one open-source build tool. This page lists every source with its licence; the same list is in the app under How GlobeVote works → Credits.

Earth imagery and geodata

  • Blue Marble: Next Generation — surface colour. NASA Earth Observatory / NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, image by Reto Stöckli, via NASA Visible Earth. Public domain.
  • Black Marble 2012 — city lights on the night side. NASA Earth Observatory / NOAA National Geophysical Data Center, Suomi NPP VIIRS data. Public domain.
  • Blue Marble cloud composite — the drifting cloud layer. NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Public domain.
  • GEBCO 2008 grid — mountains and sea floor, converted by us into the relief and water maps. GEBCO Compilation Group, as distributed by NASA Visible Earth. Public domain; citation “GEBCO Compilation Group (2008) GEBCO 2008 Grid”.
  • NASA GIBS tiles — land tiles at about 2 km per pixel built into the app, and finer ones downloaded only if “Sharper ground” is switched on: Blue Marble Next Generation and VIIRS City Lights 2012 tiles from NASA’s Global Imagery Browse Services. Public domain.
  • Natural Earth — land outlines for the fallback globe. Made with Natural Earth, free vector and raster map data at naturalearthdata.com. Public domain.

NASA, NOAA, GEBCO and Natural Earth do not endorse GlobeVote.

Formulas and hand-made data

  • Sun position from the low-precision solar formulas of the Astronomical Almanac; moon phase from the J2000.0 new-moon epoch and the mean synodic month.
  • Country centres compiled by hand to about a degree; country names from your device’s locale; flags from Unicode regional-indicator emoji; our own country-to-region mapping, loosely after the UN M49 geoscheme.

Apple frameworks

SwiftUI, SceneKit and Metal shader modifiers, Swift Charts, CloudKit, CryptoKit, MultipeerConnectivity, Combine, Foundation, UIKit and SF Symbols, under the Apple SDK and SF Symbols licences.

Build tooling (never shipped)

XcodeGen (MIT licence) generates the Xcode project. Release, screenshot and texture scripts are our own. This website is hand-written HTML with system fonts, hosted on GitHub Pages, with no analytics or third-party scripts.
