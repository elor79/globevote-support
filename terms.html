GlobeVote is published by Connectix GmbH, Switzerland ("we"). By using the app you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

What GlobeVote is

A conversation game: ask a question, let people answer, see the result on a globe. Polls you keep on your device or share with people nearby are yours alone. Polls you publish to the world are shown to every GlobeVote user through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Publishing is off by default and needs your explicit switch for each poll.

Ground rules for the world globe

Every published question and every answer must follow these rules. They are shown in the app before you publish.

  • Ask in good faith. No harassment, hate, threats, or humiliation of anyone, in the room or outside it.
  • No personal data. Do not ask for or reveal addresses, contact details, health information, or anything about a named private person.
  • Label mature themes as 18+ so they stay behind the age confirmation. Sexual content involving minors, or anything illegal in Switzerland or your country, is never allowed.
  • No spam, no advertising, no vote manipulation. One answer per iCloud account per poll is enforced by the server; do not try to work around it.

Reporting, blocking, removal

Anyone can Report & hide a conversation from its menu; it disappears from that device at once. A world poll that receives three reports from different iCloud accounts is hidden for everyone automatically. Anyone can block a host, after which nothing that host publishes is shown on that device. There is no human moderator watching the feed; we act on reports sent to info@elorenz.ch within 24 hours and may remove any content or exclude any account from the world globe at our discretion.

Your content

You keep all rights to what you write. By publishing you grant us and every GlobeVote user a licence to display it inside the app for as long as it stays published. You can unpublish a poll at any time, and "Delete my world data" in the app removes everything your iCloud account put on the world globe.

Age

The world globe is for people aged 16 and over. Conversations marked 18+ by their host are only shown after a one-time confirmation that you are an adult. We do not verify age and rely on your honesty and on the App Store's age rating.

No warranty

GlobeVote is provided as is, free of charge. Results are informal and count iCloud accounts, not people; they do not represent public opinion and must not be used as if they did. We may change or discontinue the world globe at any time.
